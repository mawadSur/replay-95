# Replay '95 — Product Brief

> Authoritative product brief. The per-segment review agents in `.claude/agents/` read this file when comparing what's built against the intended experience.

**App Name:** Replay '95
**Tagline:** Your 90s life. Reloaded.
**Target audience:** Born 1980–1995
**Core emotion:** Not just nostalgia — interactive time travel through the lens of your actual younger self.

---

## 1. Core Concept

Replay '95 is a personalized nostalgia journal + daily interactive experience that rebuilds the 90s as if you're living it again — but through today's technology.

It integrates:

- Your actual historical data (birth year, hometown, first crush's music taste, etc.)
- Real 90s media snippets (music, TV, ads, news)
- Social rituals modeled on pre-internet social life

No infinite scrolling. Instead, you check in once or twice a day for short, immersive "episodes" of your re-lived youth.

---

## 2. Detailed Features

### A. Onboarding: "Build Your 90s Avatar"

New users complete a nostalgic questionnaire (fun, 5–7 min):

- What was your first Walkman / CD / cassette?
- Which mall store did you beg to enter?
- Were you SNES, Sega Genesis, or Nintendo 64?
- Did you watch TGIF, SNICK, or Toonami?
- First concert or dream concert in 1995–1999

This creates a 90s profile that personalizes the entire experience.

The avatar looks like a school photo from 1995 (choose outfit, Trapper Keeper pattern, slap bracelet color).

### B. Daily "Memory Channel"

Each day at 7 PM (or user-chosen time), users receive a push notification styled as a TV Guide listing:

> "Tonight at 7:30 — 'Friday Night Videos: 1994 Alternative Rock Special.' Complete the challenge to save your spot on the couch."

Inside an episode:

- A 60–90 second clip montage of the day's theme (e.g., "Songs you slow-danced to" or "Best cereal mascots")
- 3 interactive prompts based on the user's profile:
  - "You're 13. Your mom drops you and a friend at the mall for 2 hours. Where do you go first?"
  - "Rate how accurate this memory is: Trying to record a song from the radio without the DJ talking."
- Reply via voice memo or text. (Short video is **post-beta**; period-accurate filters live in the camera/booth flow only — see §2.C.)

### C. AR "VHS Viewer"

A camera mode that overlays virtual 90s artifacts onto real spaces:

- Point at a couch → see a 90s kid lying upside down watching TV
- Point at a landline phone → caller ID box shows "Mom calling… pick up!"
- Point at a car seat → a realistic car lighter and cassette adapter appear

These AR moments are shareable as still images or 15-second loops without leaving the app.

### D. "Classroom Notes" Social Layer

No comment sections. Instead, users send virtual folded notes:

- Choose paper style (Lisa Frank, ripped notebook, Pogs backing)
- Write a short message (140 chars max — pre-Twitter)
- Fold it and "pass" to a friend in the app

Notes arrive with a delay (30 min to 6 hours), mimicking passing notes between classes. Users can also send to a "pen pal" match based on shared 90s interests — high trust, low pressure.

### E. Weekly "Saturday Morning" Quests

Each weekend unlocks a multi-step nostalgic scavenger hunt:

1. "Find a cereal box in your pantry and recreate the 90s commercial jingle"
2. "Make a friendship bracelet and scan it — get a sticker pack"
3. "Name 5 toys from the back of a cereal box"

Completing quests earns digital pogs (in-app currency) to redeem for:

- 90s fonts for your profile
- Unlockable AR decorations for your "bedroom" (a static but customizable 90s bedroom diorama)
- Exclusive retro mini-games: Sock 'em on SNICK, Mall Madness Dash, Trapper Keeper Tycoon

## 3. Monetization (Respectful & Optional)

- **Free tier:** Memory Channel + limited AR + 3 notes/week
- **Replay+ ($3.99/mo or $29.99/yr):** Unlimited notes, full AR lens library, access to "Saturday Morning Quest" Vault (past quests), no ads for partner content.
- **Classified Ads section** (intentionally cheesy): Vintage 90s-style classifieds for users to trade nostalgia items (IRL or digital). Sponsored by retro brands (e.g., Polaroid, Zima, Surge). 10% fee on IRL marketplace sales.
- **Premium limited drops:** Officially licensed 90s brand AR stickers (e.g., a working virtual Tamagotchi you keep in your "backpack" in the app).

---

## 4. Unique Positioning for 1980–1995 Borns

Too young for Woodstock, too old to be TikTok natives. They remember:

- Life before the internet (just barely)
- The transition from analog to digital (AOL CDs, Napster, first cell phones)
- Deep emotional ties to physical objects (tapes, ticket stubs, yearbooks)

Replay '95 respects that by:

- Not gamifying everything — some moments are quiet, just shared notes
- Avoiding algorithmic feeds — feels curated like a zine, not a firehose
- Encouraging real-world reflection — AR points at your space, not someone else's highlight reel

---

## 5. Launch Strategy

- **Soft-launch beta:** Limited to 5,000 users via Facebook 90s groups + Reddit (r/90s, r/Xennials)
- **Waitlist mechanic:** "Remember waiting for your turn on the family computer? Same energy."
- **First 90 days theme:** Summer of '94 — daily content tied to summer 1994 (Woodstock '94, Forrest Gump, World Cup, Pulp Fiction teaser, AOL 2.5 launch)
